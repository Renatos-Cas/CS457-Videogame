 1. Do NOT produce generic socket boilerplate. Never assume one recv() call returns exactly one message.
 2. Do NOT add anything that you didn't find here. Implement only what this specification defines.
 3. Do NOT guess anything that you don't know. Before writing code, ask.
 4. For transport and framing, follow what is listed in the SOW Document CS457.md file.
 5. For the message types, follow what is listed in the protocol_blueprint.md file.
